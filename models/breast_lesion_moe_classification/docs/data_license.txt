Data and third-party model terms for the breast_lesion_moe_classification bundle.
No images and no datasets are redistributed in this bundle.

BRACS (BReAst Carcinoma Subtyping) -- training and test data
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://www.bracs.icar.cnr.it
BRACS is a public dataset provided by its maintainers for research use.
Obtain it from the maintainers and place it under `data/` as described in
https://github.com/eliem08/paper_code (README, "Data" section). The official
patient-disjoint train/val/test split is used; case lists are in that
repository's `artifacts/manifests/`.

BACH (ICIAR 2018 Grand Challenge) and BreaKHis -- evaluation cohorts
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Used in the paper for cross-cohort / out-of-distribution evaluation only.
Obtain them from their maintainers; they are not included here.

Pathology foundation models (UNI, Virchow2, Phikon-v2) -- NOT distributed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The embeddings this bundle consumes are produced by third-party foundation
models that are gated on Hugging Face: accept their terms and set `HF_TOKEN`
in your environment (see https://github.com/eliem08/paper_code, README,
"Data" section). Those models are subject to their providers' terms and are
not distributed with this bundle. The domain-adapted (fine-tuned) Phikon-v2
expert weights are not currently public.
